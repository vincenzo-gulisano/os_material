## Group Allocation Schedule

This page explains how student groups are handled for the Operating Systems course and provides the schedule for allocating and finalizing groups.

### Timeline

| ID | Date | Event |
|---|---|---|
| A | Wednesday 2026/09/02 | This document is shared with students, with a note that it will be explained in detail during the first lecture on Thursday 2026/09/03. |
| B | Wednesday 2026/09/02 | Initial groups, each with three members, are created. If the number of registered students is not a multiple of three, some groups will have two members. **NOTE:** This is not the final grouping for the course. At this point, there is no guarantee that the people each student is paired with are the ones that student will work with. Groups are finalized during the following week. |
| C | Thursday 2026/09/03 | This document is discussed live during the first lecture. |
| D | Friday 2026/09/04 23:59:59 — **DEADLINE** | Deadline for students to **individually** indicate in Canvas that they acknowledge they have read this document and seen their group assignment. If a student does not complete this step, the examiner assumes that the student is not interested in completing the labs as of now (e.g., because the student is taking the course again to prepare for the exam but has already completed the labs). |
| E | Monday 2026/09/07 | All students who have acknowledged by the deadline but are still part of a group with fewer than three members are compacted into groups of three. If the number of registered students is not a multiple of three, some groups will have two members. **NOTE:** This is the final grouping for the course. |
| F | Wednesday 2026/09/09 23:59:59 — **DEADLINE** | Groups upload their contracts in Canvas. |
